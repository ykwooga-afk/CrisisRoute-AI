# CrisisRoute AI — Two-Minute Pitch

Main narration target: approximately 115–120 seconds. Speak calmly; do not rush the CASE 03 insight.

## Main Script

### 0:00–0:15 — Messy reports

**[Screen: five-case Command Center]**

“During a haze or fire-smoke emergency, reports arrive as incomplete messages, duplicate forwards, and urgent claims with missing details. Treating every report as simply true or false can hide the people who need attention most.”

### 0:15–0:30 — The core idea

**[Screen: three score axes]**

“CrisisRoute AI does not decide whether a report is simply true or false. It separates verification, urgency and actionability so incomplete but dangerous reports are not ignored. Low verification does not mean low urgency.”

### 0:30–0:50 — Why Gonka

**[Screen: Analyst and Reviewer panel]**

“Through Gonka Router, DeepSeek acts as the Analyst while MiniMax performs an independent blind review. Both receive the same original evidence, but MiniMax never sees DeepSeek’s answer. Their structured scores are validated, then deterministic code calculates consensus and conflict.”

### 0:50–1:10 — CASE 03

**[Open CASE 03 and Safety Gates]**

“CASE 03 reports an elderly person with breathing difficulty. Evidence is weak, but the possible harm is immediate. CrisisRoute keeps urgency high while location and contact gates block dispatch. The safe next step is urgent verification, not ignoring the report and not pretending rescue was sent.”

### 1:10–1:30 — CASE 01

**[Open CASE 01 and Human Decision]**

“CASE 01 has stronger corroboration, a usable location, and available resources. Even here, AI cannot execute action. A human chooses an allowed decision, confirms the safety acknowledgements, and the system records `RECORDED` while execution remains `NOT_EXECUTED`.”

### 1:30–1:47 — Brief and proof

**[Generate Brief; verify Proof]**

“Deterministic code generates the operational brief and a local Proof Capsule. The capsule detects payload tampering, but it is not blockchain proof and does not prove the report itself is true.”

### 1:47–2:00 — Public value

**[Return to five-case overview]**

“CrisisRoute helps communities triage noisy information without automating life-critical authority. It preserves uncertainty, surfaces urgent harm, and makes the operational boundary visible. AI assists. Humans decide.”

## Backup Closing — Under 20 Seconds

“CrisisRoute separates verification, urgency, and actionability, then keeps every operational step behind deterministic safety gates and a human decision. It does not dispatch rescue or claim blockchain proof. AI assists. Humans decide.”
